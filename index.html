Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

What is chiropractic care?

Chiropractic is a primary healthcare profession that focuses on the relationship between the spine and the nervous system. Chiropractors (registered with the Chiropractic Association of Ireland), use precise adjustments to improve spinal function, which supports the body’s natural ability to adapt and heal.

Is chiropractic care safe?

Yes. Chiropractic is recognized as an exceptionally safe, non-invasive, and drug-free approach for neuromusculoskeletal complaints. CAI members are highly trained to perform thorough examinations to ensure treatment is appropriate for you.

What happens during the first visit?

Your initial consultation typically lasts 30–60 minutes. It includes a full case history, orthopaedic and neurological examinations, and a functional analysis of your spine and nervous system.

How many sessions will I need?

Every individual is unique. Some minor issues resolve in a few visits, while chronic conditions may require a longer stabilization phase ranging from weeks to months.

We focus on personalised care, not lengthy upfront commitments. We'll work together to find the right number of sessions for your needs, ensuring you feel comfortable and in control of your progress.

Does the adjustment hurt?

Chiropractic adjustments are generally gentle and not painful. Some patients may feel brief discomfort if the area is already very inflamed, but most find the treatment highly relieving.

What conditions do chiropractors treat?

Beyond back and neck pain, chiropractors treat headaches, migraines, sciatica, shoulder tension, and various joint issues like "tennis elbow" or "jumper’s knee".

Does health insurance cover chiropractic?

Most major health insurers in Ireland provide reimbursement for chiropractic care, provided the practitioner is a member of the Chiropractic Association of Ireland (CAI). You should check your specific policy for details.
